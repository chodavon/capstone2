The Sotear training principle

A 60-second test

Would this email fool you?

Read the sample. Then tap each red flag to see what gives it away. No real bank or brand is used.

Inbox / 1 new message
From

MekongPay Support <support@mekongpay-secure.example>

To

you@yourcompany.com

Subject

Urgent: unusual sign-in on your account

Dear customer,

We blocked a sign-in from a new device. To keep your account active, confirm your identity within 2 hours.

Verify your account

If you did not request this, ignore this email.

MekongPay Customer Care

Link preview

https://mekongpay-secure.example/verify

Spot the tell

0 of 5 found

  • The domain adds extra words before the real name. A real bank writes from its official domain, not a lookalike.

  • “Urgent” and “unusual sign-in” are designed to make you act before you check. Real security alerts do not need to frighten you.

  • “Dear customer” means the same message was sent to thousands of people. A real bank can use your name.

  • Two hours is meant to stop you from calling the bank or checking the app. Slow down. Verify through a channel you chose.

  • The link does not go to the bank’s app or official site. Do not tap it. Open the app yourself, or type the address you know.

This sample is fictional. “MekongPay” is not a real service.

Questions

Straight answers.

If something is not here, ask. We will give you a plain answer, even if the answer is “you do not need that yet.”

Ask a question
Is testing safe for our live website?

We agree on a scope and test window first. We avoid destructive tests unless you approve them. For busy systems, we can test a staging copy. If we find something risky, we stop and call you.

Do we need a big IT team?

No. We work with whoever manages your systems, even if that is an outside IT provider. We explain findings in plain language, with technical detail only where your team needs it.

How long does a test take?

A single web app or small network usually takes 3–7 working days of testing, plus 3–5 days for the report. Training can be a three-hour starter or a full-day workshop. We agree dates before starting.

What do we get at the end?

A one-page summary for leaders, a risk rating for each finding, evidence, and a fix step. We also offer a debrief so your team can ask questions.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. We can sign your NDA or use a simple mutual agreement. Testing only begins after written authorization.

Do you help after the report?

Yes. We can walk your team through fixes and retest the important findings. A retest of critical fixes is included in the Focused Penetration Test.

Do you sell compliance certificates?

No. We help you find and fix real weaknesses. We can give you evidence to show a client or auditor, but we do not sell certificates. Ask for the team’s credentials and a sample report before you sign with any provider.
